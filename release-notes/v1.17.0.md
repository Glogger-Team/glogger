## What's Changed since v1.16.0

### Improved
- - The app now reads the new VIP JSON entries in Chat.log and character reports and no longer looks at Player.log.
- - While Glogger does what it can to parse everything you get from free logs, but it's MUCH better if you have the VIP logging.
- - A new Combat view shows kills, deaths, experience, and Combat Wisdom. Kill loot links to the kill that dropped it.
- - Farming view is now historic-only, going to drop it at some point.
- - The Chat view has a JSON tab. It shows those new VIP json chat lines if you want to see them but also keeps them out of the way of the rest of the chat logging.
- - The app once again tracks Words of Power!
- - Removed non-working milking timers of NPC cows
- - Statehelm gifting status is back!
- - Switching characters now resets combat totals and reloads death records.
- - Re-imports should no longer duplicate deaths or experience.
- - The bonus split shows unavailable on chat-only data instead of zero. The Milking Timers tab is hidden until writers are rewired.
- - Work continues on the Survey tracking, but it's not all done yet. We're getting there slowly.
