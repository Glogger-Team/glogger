## What's Changed since v1.17.3

### Fixed
- The Arena widget now shows fights that finished while the app was closed. Restart the app and the new results appear with no reload.
- Re-reading a chat log file no longer duplicates its events. The Chat tabs showed each JSON event and status line again with each re-read. This update also removes the duplicates that past re-reads stored.
- The Roulette and Combat Wisdom widgets now refresh with results that arrive during startup. They showed stale results until reload when the app ingested history after launch.
