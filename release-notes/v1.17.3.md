## What's Changed since v1.17.2

### Improved
- v1.17.3
- - The Arena widget now shows today only. It has three tabs: Today, Full Day's Results, and Lifetime Results.
- - The Today tab lists the five daily tipsters. A tipster with no tip yet shows a blank row.
- - The Today tab also shows each fighter's win-loss record for today. Lifetime Results shows all-time records.
- - The parser now reads all observed Kuzavek result lines. It also reads elongated fighter names.
- - Dead betting paths are pruned. Today's hot tips surface in the widget.
- - Agent retro tooling gates are in place.
